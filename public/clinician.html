About these measures

Every two weeks, people who said yes answer three validated questionnaires, one question per card, with the published wording and answer options: WHO-5 wellbeing (0–100), PHQ-8 depression symptoms (0–24) and the Three-Item Loneliness Scale (3–9). The PHQ-8 is the PHQ-9 without the question about self-harm, because nobody watches the answers in real time. The status comes from fixed rules. Might need a call: PHQ-8 of 15 or more, or up 5+ points since their first check-in. Keep an eye on: PHQ-8 of 10 or more, WHO-5 of 50 or less or down 10+ points since their first check-in, loneliness of 6 or more, no check-in for three weeks, “no” to every suggestion in two weeks, or two meet-ups that felt flat. AI summaries see only the numbers: no name, place, partner or date.
